# Her weekly cleaning routine (original notes, verbatim)

Weekly cleaning routine

Living room, dining room, entry way, stairs, mud room area, laundry room (main), dog room, outdoor (front and back), garage, office, pantries, kitchen, master, master closet, master bath, hallway on parents side, hallway on kids side, playroom, willows room, Timmy's room, girls bath, littles bath, nursery, Truman room, Truman's bath, upstairs, half bath (down), stairs

Monday
- Laundry (twins clothes, Truman clothes)
- Meal prep
- Grocery order
- Crockpot meal

Tuesday (bedrooms)
- Laundry bedding (and towels if didn't get to it on Monday)
- Bedroom: new sheets, tidy, dust, wipe down surfaces, clean glass, wipe down blinds, vacuum
- Turn on scents, spray beds with allergy spray, spray air fresher, fabric spray

Wednesday (living room, dining room, entry way, stairs)
- Tidy
- Dust
- Wipe down surfaces
- Clean glass
- Polish wood surfaces
- Wipe down electronics
- Vacuum/vac mop
- Spray scents, allergy spray, fabric spray
- Mop (if not using vacuum mop)
- Adults and little girl laundry

Thursday (theatre room, office, game room)
- Tidy
- Dust
- Wipe down electronics

Friday
- Bathroom: tidy, remove items from shower to clean shower
- Laundry: bathroom rugs, towels
- Moms laundry

Daily
- Start laundry
- Make all beds
- Tidy bedrooms
- Tidy in kitchen (unload dishwasher)
- Clean out sink
- Wipe down the counters in the kitchen
- Tidy living room
- Vacuum living room
- Vacuum dog room (where dogs are)
- Wipe shower or rinse tubs (spray method shower cleaner)
- Spray scent and allergy, fabric spray in the living room
